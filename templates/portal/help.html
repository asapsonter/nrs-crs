Maker and Checker

A Maker prepares filings; a Checker reviews and submits them to the NRS. This separation of duties keeps a second pair of eyes on every return before it leaves the institution. The Primary User is the first Checker and can add further users under Manage Users.

Preparing a return

  1. Open Submissions and choose CRS XML upload, manual entry, or a nil return.
  2. For an XML upload, the Portal validates the file against the CRS schema on upload.
  3. A Maker stages the filing; a Checker then reviews and submits it to the NRS.
  4. If the NRS returns a filing for correction, it appears under Draft Filings for you to amend and resubmit.

Need assistance

For enrolment changes or questions the Portal cannot answer, contact the NRS AEOI-CRS unit. Keep your Reporting Entity reference to hand.
